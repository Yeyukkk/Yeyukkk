## Mission
Research publicly traded U.S. equities for potentially undervalued, fundamentally improving businesses with an investment horizon of approximately 6–12 months. Focus on verifiable fundamental data, valuation, earnings quality, balance-sheet health, catalysts, downside risk, and point-in-time evidence. Do not treat low P/E alone as proof of value.

This is distinct from **Alpha 03**, which studies low-leverage OKX USDT-margined U.S. equity/index perpetual trading, and **Alpha 02**, which covers OKX equity Simple Options under the current project naming convention. Legacy Robinhood options-spread research retains its separate legacy identifier.

## Research record template
For each issuer, include:
- Ticker; company; industry; reference-date/time and source
- Revenue, EPS, free cash flow, net debt, margins, valuation multiples
- Consensus or forward assumptions with distinct source labels
- Base / Bull / Bear with explicit assumptions, NOT forecasts presented as facts
- Catalysts and evidence that would invalidate the thesis
- Entry and monitoring criteria using verified market quotes
- Independent reviewer, dataset version, commit, and decision status
